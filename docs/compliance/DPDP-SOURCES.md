# DPDP legal sources

Technical interpretation uses the [Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (official India Code record)](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/22064) and the final notified Rules as published through the [Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology](https://www.meity.gov.in/) and [e-Gazette of India](https://egazette.nic.in/). The January 2025 draft is not an implementation source.

Exact notice Gazette number, translations, consent basis mapping, grievance language, and applicability must be confirmed by counsel against the signed Gazette artifact. No text here is a certification or legal opinion.
